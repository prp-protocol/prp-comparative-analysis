# Scope and rights status

This initial public subset prepared for publication contains project-authored explanatory documentation, an unchanged project annotation module and integrity identifiers. It excludes third-party papers, standards, artwork, vendored source, binaries, mixed research datasets and private operational records.

No blanket license or new permission grant is made here. No open-source or openly licensed status is asserted. The owner must separately choose any code and documentation licenses and their exact scope before such grants are added. A choice to make the repository visible without an additional reuse grant must not be represented as an open-source release.

The licenses and notices of excluded evidence remain with their original bytes in private custody. Public availability of a source does not establish redistribution permission. Existing notices for individual assets do not clear unrelated material.

Rights review of additional source-specific material is separate from publication of this minimal project-authored subset. No whole-corpus clearance is a prerequisite for considering this bounded subset.
