# Provenance and recovery limits

Integrity references in provenance.json identify accepted bytes, not public downloads. The full dataset, primary sources, source/path mapping, signed delivery objects and validation receipts are retained in access-controlled research custody outside this public tree. Public users must not be told that internal filesystem paths are accessible links.

Forty-five historical original catalog URLs were withdrawn after verified private custody substitution. They are not maintained or restored by this repository. Four original objects supporting the recorded live 7.96 baseline remain served under the existing authorized arrangements. The migrated private custody contains the earlier objects and their signatures.

Seventeen old scripts still require path or catalog adaptation. Unmodified historical replay is not preserved. Historical results and recipes remain unchanged; adapting operational paths is a future scoped task. Previously removed snapshots, downloads and build caches remain documented gaps.

Authorized recovery uses the private digest-to-custody index, retained source manifest and resolver, archived detached signatures, and the catalog public key with fingerprint BDF794EB7E1CBDE115BDC13B29F9A1FA8EA34B8A. Public-key filename mapping is retained privately. Hash and signature checks establish the bytes being recovered; they do not recreate an original URL or grant public redistribution rights.

This public subset includes no access tokens, private paths, signed evidence bodies or source-manifest contents. Questions about unavailable evidence should identify the revision and public integrity identifier through the project's established review channel; access is not promised.
