# Research and publication ownership

prp-comparative-analysis owns the research dataset, rationale, evidence levels, research queue and source custody. prp-editorial owns presentation, page management and publication decisions. prp-website performs website integration and deployment through the established workflow.

For a separately authorized update, the research owner supplies an exact revision, data-only export, SHA-256 and validation/qualification summary. Editorial reviews that snapshot and coordinates the website workflow. Website templates, controls and deployment remain distinct from research decisions. There is no automatic synchronization of research drafts.

Current accepted research is 7.97: 912 Yes, 769 No and 55 Pending. Last recorded live baseline is 7.96: 911 Yes, 769 No and 56 Pending. The minimal public subset does not contain either dataset and does not deploy 7.97.

The private workspace retains the existing standard-library export pipeline. Its operational commands belong to that access-controlled workspace; a public checkout must not be presented as containing its inputs. Future distribution of a reviewed dataset is a separate finite content decision.

Preserve stable /comparison/ links, active criterion and stack identifiers, source qualifications and the approved removal of retired criterion material. Migration does not authorize restoring historical archives, changing verdicts or republishing third-party evidence.
